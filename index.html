What about .exe, .apk and .ipa?

Those are compiled programs, and a web page cannot build one — it would need a compiler and a signing key on your computer, not in a browser tab. Nobody's engine does it from inside the browser either; they all hand the job to a tool you run yourself. The good news is the .html above is exactly what those tools take:

· .exe (Windows) — drop the .html into Electron or Tauri and build.
· .apk (Android) — Capacitor wraps it; Android Studio makes the .apk.
· .ipa (iPhone) — Capacitor again, but it also needs a Mac and a paid Apple developer account. That part is Apple's rule, not ours.

On a phone, the .html can also be added to the home screen from the browser menu, which gets you an icon that opens straight into the game without any of that.
